# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Experimental musicians and sound designers, initially the project’s creator,
using the sequencer during focused patch exploration and live performance. They
need to discover unusual geometric rhythms, preserve breakthroughs, and move
between viable states without losing musical continuity.

## Product Purpose

Turn projective geometry, orbital motion, and quasilattice structure into a
playable MIDI instrument. Success means the underlying system can remain strange
and generative while its best regions are recoverable, navigable, and safe to
perform.

## Brand Personality

Austere, technical, cosmic. Precise enough to trust, unusual enough to invite
experimentation, and quiet enough that the geometry remains the focal point.

## Anti-references

Avoid faux-analog hardware skeuomorphism, decorative glassmorphism, generic
plugin chrome, gratuitous neon, and dense control surfaces with no distinction
between sound design and performance actions.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the wild topology; provide reliable paths through it.
- Make performance gestures lower-dimensional than sound-design gestures.
- Keep a recoverable home state visible and immediately reachable.
- Expose discontinuities and queued changes instead of hiding them.
- Let geometric and musical state, not decoration, carry the visual identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Every operation must remain keyboard accessible with a
visible focus state. Honor reduced-motion preferences and never rely on color
alone to communicate state.
